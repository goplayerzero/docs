## Run PlayerZero Work On Your Own Machines

Connect your local machine to PlayerZero to make it a "Base" where PlayerZero can execute work next to the container images, compilers, linters, test suites and real configuration that it needs to do the job to your standards. Each Base belongs to an individual person or to a Fleet. Personal bases are never shared (just like personal laptops are never shared) while Fleets support running shared, automated workflows even while most individuals are offline. Uncommitted work moves between a PlayerZero thread and a machine, or between two machines, in either direction, and you answer any collision before it lands.

A Player and your local toolchain now make each other more effective, rather than one person relaying build and test output from one to the other by hand. A Fleet extends that to shared machines that stay reachable when a laptop is closed, so larger builds, longer test suites and overnight automations keep running.

## Find All of Your Work in One Project Inbox

Your Inbox is a project-level view of the work that involves you. It covers every workflow in the project alongside the channels and questions that belong to no workflow, including the questions you asked PlayerZero directly. Active Channels holds your channels, approvals and questions from the last thirty days, and narrows to Needs Input or Workflow Approvals. Completed Workflow lists finished runs, so you can reopen one and read what it concluded. Saved Channels keeps the channels you bookmark, including a run still in flight. The workflow inboxes pinned to your sidebar remain, and Manage Inboxes sets the default view for each one.

Finding your own work no longer depends on remembering which workflow it belonged to, or whether it belonged to one at all.

## View Artifacts Full Page in Design View

Members can now open a thread's artifact in a full-page view and choose which version to read from a version picker. The view supports Markdown, HTML, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and CSV or TSV files.

A report, deck or design that a thread produced can now be reviewed at full size.

---

## Preview Knowledge Base Documents and See Where They Came From

Knowledge base documents now open in a preview beside the document list. Each document also shows its version, its source (Upload, Agent, Workflow or System), when it was last updated and who wrote it.

Admins and members can check what their agents are reading, and whether a person or a workflow run wrote it.

---

## See How Long Workflows Take and What Each Run Costs

Building on the workflow analytics introduced in August, the workflow detail view in Usage Analytics now shows "Avg time to complete".

Admins and workflow owners can weigh what a workflow costs against how long it takes, using measured figures rather than estimates.
